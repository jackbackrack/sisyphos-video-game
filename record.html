Sisyphus's sounds

Record your own grunts, screams, boulder rumble, wheel and horse sounds for the game. Each take is trimmed and levelled automatically. The game uses your takes instead of the built-in ones, in this browser, as soon as you go back to it.

To put them in the shared version of the game, download the takes you like (or all of them) and send the files over to be bundled in. Back to the game

Grunts

Short effort sounds as you shove the boulder: “hnngh”, “ugh”, “hrrrh”. Record several (6–12 is good) so they vary.
